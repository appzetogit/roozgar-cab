B. What ZI CAB Does
ZI CAB is a technology platform that connects riders who need transport or parcel delivery with independent drivers and vehicle owners who provide those services. Services on the ZI CAB Platform may include city rides by bike, auto, cab and other vehicle types, outstation and rental trips, and parcel delivery, depending on your city.
Drivers on the ZI CAB Platform are independent service providers and are not employees or agents of ZI CAB. The transport service itself is provided by the driver; ZI CAB provides the technology to book, track, pay for and support that service.

C. Definitions
"Rider" means a person who books a ride or parcel delivery through the ZI CAB Platform.
"Driver" means a person, and where applicable the vehicle owner, registered on the ZI CAB Platform to provide rides or deliveries.
"Trip" means a ride or delivery booked through the ZI CAB Platform, from acceptance by a driver until completion or cancellation.
"Fare" means the amount payable by the rider for a trip, including any applicable taxes, tolls, parking and other charges shown in the app.
"Wallet" means the ZI CAB in-app wallet used for payments, refunds, earnings and fees.

D. Eligibility and Your Account
You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a contract under Indian law to create an account. Minors may travel only on a booking made and supervised by a parent or guardian.
You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time password (OTP). You are responsible for all activity on your account. Do not share your OTP with anyone - ZI CAB will never ask you for it by phone.
The information you give us must be true, complete and kept up to date. We may suspend or close accounts that use false information, belong to someone else, or are used for fraud.
Each person may hold only one rider account and one driver account.

E. Booking a Trip
When you enter your pickup and drop locations and choose a vehicle type, the app shows you the fare before you confirm. By confirming, you ask ZI CAB to find a nearby driver for you.
A booking is confirmed only when a driver accepts it. The app then shows the driver's name, photo, vehicle number, rating and live location.
Every trip has a ride OTP. Share it with the driver only after you have checked that the vehicle number matches the app and you are inside the correct vehicle. The trip starts only once the correct OTP is entered.
ZI CAB does not guarantee that a driver will be available for every request, or the time a driver will take to arrive.

F. Fares and Adding to Your Fare
Fares are calculated from the vehicle type, distance, estimated time and the pricing set for your city and zone. The fare shown before booking may change if you change the drop location, add stops, wait beyond the free waiting time, or take a significantly different route at your request.
Tolls, parking fees, state entry taxes and similar charges paid during the trip are added to the fare where applicable.
Where the option is available, you may add a fixed amount to your fare while you wait for a driver, to make your request more attractive. The new fare is shown before you add it, applies only to that trip, and cannot exceed the limit shown in the app. The amount you add is part of the fare and is not refundable once a driver accepts at that fare.
For outstation, rental and night trips, additional charges such as driver allowance, night charges and extra kilometre or extra hour charges may apply as shown in the app before booking.

G. Payments
You can pay by cash, UPI, card, net banking or ZI CAB Wallet, as available in your city. Online payments are processed by licensed third-party payment partners; ZI CAB does not store your full card number or UPI PIN.
For cash trips, pay the driver the exact fare shown in the app at the end of the trip. Do not pay any amount that is not shown in the app.
Wallet balances are not interest-bearing, cannot be transferred to another person, and can be used only on the ZI CAB Platform, subject to applicable law.
If a payment fails or is charged twice, contact support with the trip details and we will investigate.

H. Cancellations
You can cancel a booking from the trip screen. Cancelling within a short time after booking, or when the driver is significantly delayed, is free.
A cancellation fee may apply if you cancel after the driver has travelled towards you or arrived at the pickup point, or if you do not show up at the pickup point within the waiting time. Any fee is shown in the app before you confirm the cancellation.
Drivers who repeatedly cancel accepted trips without a valid reason may be charged a fee, lose incentives, or be suspended.

I. Refunds
If you were charged incorrectly, charged for a trip that did not take place, or charged twice, contact support within 7 days of the trip.
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method or to your ZI CAB Wallet, usually within 5-7 working days depending on your bank or payment provider.
Cash payments cannot be refunded as cash; approved amounts are credited to your ZI CAB Wallet.

J. Parcel Delivery
You are responsible for the accuracy of the sender and receiver details and for describing the item correctly.
You must not send prohibited items, including cash, jewellery, precious metals, illegal drugs, alcohol where restricted, weapons, explosives, flammable or hazardous materials, live animals, perishable food not properly packed, or any item banned by law.
Items must be packed securely and must fit safely on the chosen vehicle. The driver may refuse any parcel that is unsafe, oversized or appears to contain prohibited items.
ZI CAB's liability for loss of or damage to a parcel, where proven to be caused during the trip, is limited to the lower of the declared value or Rs 5,000, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

K. Rider Conduct
Treat drivers with respect. Abuse, harassment, threats, violence or discrimination of any kind will lead to immediate suspension.
Follow traffic and safety rules, including wearing a helmet on bike rides and a seatbelt in cabs.
Do not carry or consume illegal substances, carry weapons, smoke where not allowed, or damage the vehicle. You will be charged for any damage or cleaning caused by you.
Do not ask the driver to exceed the number of passengers allowed for the vehicle, overload it, or break any law.
Do not book trips for unlawful purposes or to transport prohibited goods.

L. Driver Terms
Drivers must hold a valid driving licence for the vehicle class, and the vehicle must have a valid registration certificate, insurance, fitness certificate, pollution certificate and permit where the law requires them. All documents must be uploaded in the driver app and kept up to date.
ZI CAB verifies documents before activating a driver account and may re-verify them at any time. An account may be suspended if any document expires or is found to be false.
Drivers are independent service providers. They decide when to go online, which requests to accept, and are responsible for their own vehicle, fuel, maintenance, taxes and legal compliance.
Drivers must follow all traffic laws, drive safely, keep the vehicle clean and roadworthy, collect only the fare shown in the app, and never ask the rider for extra money.
Drivers must use the ride OTP to start each trip and must complete the trip on the route shown in the app unless the rider asks otherwise.
ZI CAB charges drivers a commission or platform fee on trips, as shown in the driver app. Fees and dues are adjusted against the driver's ZI CAB Wallet. A driver whose wallet falls below the minimum balance set by ZI CAB may not receive new trips until it is recharged.
Earnings from online payments are credited to the driver's ZI CAB Wallet and can be withdrawn to the driver's registered bank account or UPI ID, subject to verification.
While a driver is online, the driver app collects their location, including in the background, to offer nearby trips and show their position to riders. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Drivers must not share their account, allow another person to drive using their account, or use any other vehicle than the one registered.
