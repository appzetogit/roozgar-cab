PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZI CAB Technologies Pvt Ltd ("ZI CAB", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares, stores and protects your information when you use the ZI CAB rider app, the ZI CAB driver app, and our website at https://zicab.in (together, the "ZI CAB Platform").

It applies to riders who book rides or parcel deliveries, drivers and vehicle owners who offer services on the ZI CAB Platform, and visitors to our website. By using the ZI CAB Platform you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the ZI CAB Platform.

B. Information We Collect From Riders
Account details: your name, mobile number, email address (if provided), profile photo (if provided), gender (if provided) and emergency contacts you choose to add.
Trip details: pickup and drop locations, stops, route, trip time, distance, fare, vehicle type chosen, ride OTP, ratings, feedback and trip history.
Parcel details: sender and receiver names, phone numbers, addresses and a description of the item being delivered.
Payment details: payment method, transaction reference, amount and wallet balance. Card and UPI details are handled directly by our licensed payment partners; ZI CAB does not store your full card number or UPI PIN.
Support details: messages, calls, tickets and any files you share with our support team.

C. Information We Collect From Drivers and Vehicle Owners
Identity and contact details: name, mobile number, email address, photo, date of birth and address.
Documents for verification: driving licence, vehicle registration certificate (RC), vehicle insurance, permit, Aadhaar, PAN and any other document required by law or by the operating city.
Vehicle details: vehicle type, make, model, registration number and photos of the vehicle.
Payout details: bank account number, IFSC code, account holder name and UPI ID, used only to pay your earnings.
Performance details: online and offline status, trips accepted, cancelled and completed, ratings, earnings, wallet balance and incentives.

D. Location Information
Riders: we use your device location to set your pickup point, show nearby drivers, calculate fares and share your live trip with you. Location is used while you are booking and during an active trip.
Drivers: we collect your location while you are online, including when the driver app is running in the background, so that we can offer you nearby ride requests, show your live position to the rider during a trip, calculate distance and fares, and support rider and driver safety. Location collection stops when you go offline.
You can turn off location permission in your phone settings at any time, but the ZI CAB Platform cannot book or offer rides without it.

E. Device and Usage Information
We collect basic technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash logs and push notification tokens. We use this to keep the apps working, fix errors, prevent fraud and send you trip updates.

F. How We Use Your Information
To create and manage your account and verify your mobile number by OTP.
To match riders with nearby drivers and complete rides and parcel deliveries.
To calculate fares, process payments, refunds, wallet transactions and driver payouts.
To share trip status, driver details and live location with the rider, and pickup details with the driver.
To verify drivers and vehicles before they are allowed to take trips.
To provide customer support and resolve complaints, disputes and lost-item requests.
To keep the ZI CAB Platform safe, including investigating incidents and preventing fraud or misuse.
To send service messages such as OTPs, booking confirmations, receipts and important updates.
To improve our services, pricing and app performance.
To meet our obligations under applicable law and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

G. How We Share Your Information
Between riders and drivers: during a trip, the rider sees the driver's name, photo, vehicle details, rating and live location, and the driver sees the rider's name, pickup and drop points. Phone numbers are shared only as needed to complete the trip.
Service providers: we use trusted partners for payments, SMS and OTP delivery, maps and navigation, push notifications, cloud hosting and customer support. They receive only the information needed to do their job and must keep it confidential.
Legal and safety: we may share information with police, courts or government authorities when required by law, or when needed to protect the safety of our riders, drivers or the public.
Business changes: if ZI CAB is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction under the same protections.
We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

H. Data Storage and Security
Your information is stored on secure servers. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and OTP-based login to protect it. Only authorised team members who need the information to do their work can access it. No system is completely secure, so please keep your phone and OTPs private and never share your OTP with anyone who calls claiming to be from ZI CAB.

I. How Long We Keep Your Information
We keep your information for as long as your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. After that, we keep trip, payment and tax records only for the period required by law, and for resolving disputes and preventing fraud. Driver verification documents are kept while the driver is active on the ZI CAB Platform and for the period required by law afterwards.

J. Your Rights and Choices
You can view and update your profile details in the app at any time.
You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account.
You can turn off promotional notifications in the app or in your phone settings. Service messages about your trips and account will still be sent.
To delete your account or make any privacy request, email zicabofficial@gmail.com or call +91 8904343123 from your registered mobile number. We will respond within 30 days. Some records may be kept after deletion where the law requires it.

K. Children
The ZI CAB Platform is intended for users aged 18 and above. Minors may travel only when the booking is made and supervised by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

L. Third-Party Links and Services
The ZI CAB Platform may link to or use third-party services such as maps and payment pages. Their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

M. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at https://zicab.in/privacy with the date it was last updated. If we make important changes, we will notify you in the app or by SMS or email.
